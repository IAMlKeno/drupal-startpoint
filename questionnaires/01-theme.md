# Theme questionnaire (Theme Agent)

## Brand
1. Brand colors (hex if known), logo files, favicon
2. Fonts (brand fonts, or open to suggestions; self-hosted vs Google Fonts)
3. Tone and style: 3-5 adjectives (e.g. calm, bold, editorial, playful)
4. Reference sites you like and dislike, and why
5. Existing brand guidelines or design assets (Figma, PDF, style guide)

## Structure
6. Base theme: Starterkit (default), Olivero/Claro-derived, Radix, or other?
7. Layout needs: header/footer style, navigation depth, sidebars, hero areas, landing page layouts (Layout Builder?)
8. Key pages to mock up (home, listing, detail, contact, other)
9. Dark mode? Multiple color schemes?

## Quality
10. WCAG target (default 2.2 AA) and any specific needs (high contrast, reduced motion)
11. Responsive breakpoints and mobile-first priority
12. Performance budget (Lighthouse targets, image strategy)
13. Browser/device support
14. Build tooling preference (none, npm + Vite/Webpack, Tailwind, Sass)

## Review
15. How many mock-up directions per round (1-3)?
16. Anything explicitly off-limits (colors, imagery, patterns)?
