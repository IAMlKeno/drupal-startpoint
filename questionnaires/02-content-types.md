# Content type questionnaire (Content Type Agent)

## Inventory
1. List the kinds of content the site will hold (pages, articles, events, products, people, resources, etc.)
2. For each, roughly how many items now and per year?
3. Existing content or a content model to reuse or migrate from?

## Structure
4. Relationships between content (e.g. articles by author, events at venues)
5. Categorization: taxonomies, tags, filters users need
6. Media: images, video, documents, galleries, embeds? Reuse across content?
7. Flexible layouts needed (Layout Builder, Paragraphs-style components) or fixed fields?

## Workflow and access
8. Roles: who creates, edits, reviews, publishes, deletes per type?
9. Moderation states (draft, review, published, archived)? Scheduled publishing?
10. Revision history and audit needs

## Site behavior
11. Multilingual content and translation workflow
12. SEO fields (meta, canonical, social cards), URL patterns per type
13. Search: what should be searchable, facets needed?
14. Do you want suggestions for content types you have not listed? (default: yes, with reasoning)
