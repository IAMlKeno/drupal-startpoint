Draft Ordinance: Rent Stabilization Notice Provision

📋 Policy Type: Housing & Tenants
📅 Posted: September 20, 2026
💬 12 Comments

Overview

The City Council is proposing a draft ordinance to strengthen tenant protections under the rent stabilization framework. This ordinance specifically addresses notice periods required for rent increases and provides additional safeguards for vulnerable tenant populations.

This page is open for registered community members to review, discuss, and submit feedback. Your input directly informs policy decision-making.

Problem Statement

Housing stability is a critical concern in our community. Current rent stabilization laws require a 30-day notice for rent increases, but tenants report that insufficient notice makes it difficult to plan housing transitions or appeal proposed increases.

Proposed Changes

Core Amendment: Increase required notice period for rent increases from 30 days to 60 days, with extended timelines (90 days) for tenants aged 65+ or with documented disabilities.

Key Provisions

  • Section 1: Notice Period — Establishes 60-day minimum notice for all rent increase notices
  • Section 2: Vulnerable Population Protection — 90-day notice for seniors and disabled tenants
  • Section 3: Notice Contents — Requires clear information about tenant rights and appeal processes
  • Section 4: Enforcement — Establishes penalties for non-compliance ($500-$1,500 per violation)

Expected Impact

PolicyLink Nexus analysis suggests this ordinance would provide immediate relief to approximately 45,000 renters in the jurisdiction. Extended notice periods are particularly important for elderly residents, families with children, disabled tenants, and all renters seeking to engage in advocacy.
